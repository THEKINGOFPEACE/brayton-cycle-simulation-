BRAYTON CYCLE SIMULATION FOR JET ENGINE

An interactive engineering simulation modeling air intake, multi-stage compression, constant-pressure combustion, turbine work extraction, and exhaust nozzle thrust generation with synchronized 3D visualization and MATLAB/Simulink integration.

🌀 1. Compressor

Calculates isentropic and actual pressure rise, outlet total temperature (Tt3), and mechanical shaft power demand (Pc) across multiple axial compression stages.

🔥 2. Combustion Chamber

Solves thermochemical energy balance, fuel-to-air ratio (FAR), fuel mass flow rate, combustor aerodynamic pressure loss, and target Turbine Inlet Temperature (TIT).

⚡ 3. Gas Turbine

Extracts mechanical shaft power to drive the compressor under strict rotational equilibrium: Pt × ηm = Pc, determining turbine outlet pressure (Pt5) and temperature (Tt5).

💨 4. Exhaust Nozzle

Determines critical sonic choking conditions, accelerating hot exhaust gases to generate gross momentum thrust and exit static pressure thrust.

2. Modeling Assumptions & Methodological Rigor

  • International Standard Atmosphere (ISA) temperature lapse (-6.5 K/km) and barometric formula up to 20,000 m.
  • Calorically imperfect distinction between cold air (γ = 1.40, cp = 1004.5 J/kg·K) and combustion gas (γ = 1.33, cp = 1150 J/kg·K).
  • Combustor energy balance solves fuel-to-air ratio strictly from fuel chemical LHV and enthalpy increase.
  • Mechanical shaft transmission enforces strict equilibrium between turbine power output and compressor power demand.
  • Exhaust nozzle automatically evaluates sonic choking conditions and includes momentum and pressure thrust terms.
